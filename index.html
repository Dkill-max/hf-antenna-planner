HF Antenna & Transformer Planner

An Excel workbook that picks an HF antenna, mounting height, feed method and feedpoint transformer (balun/unun) for your operating goal, then gives you a build checklist and a list of things to avoid.

Download the workbook (.xlsx)

Open it in Microsoft Excel or LibreOffice Calc. The dropdowns and formulas don't work in browser previews.

Quick start

  1. Transformer Calculator: enter station power, safety margin, system impedance and acceptable SWR (once).
  2. Antenna Selector: pick a goal from the dropdown and enter your operating frequency.
  3. Read the recommendation. The yellow Summary row is the one-line answer.
  4. Check What to Avoid, then work through Build Plan.
  5. After install, measure feedpoint impedance with an analyzer and enter it to confirm the transformer.

Good to know

  1. All lengths, heights and impedances are rules of thumb. Cut wire long, then trim for SWR.
  2. Measured impedances give the most reliable transformer choice.
  3. Keep antennas clear of power lines. End-fed wire ends carry high RF voltage. Observe RF exposure limits at high power.
