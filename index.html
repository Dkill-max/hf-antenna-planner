      Once installed, it works without an internet connection.

      How to use

      1. Set your station power and system impedance on this tab (once).
      2. On Antenna, pick what you want to work and enter your main frequency.
      3. Optional: choose a specific antenna to see how well it fits the goal.
      4. Read the recommendation. The amber display shows the one-line answer.
      5. Open Build to check what to avoid, then tick off the build plan.
      6. After install, measure the feedpoint impedance with an analyzer and enter it to confirm the transformer. Trim wire for lowest SWR.
      7. For an 80/60/40 m NVIS build, use the NVIS tab for per-band lengths.

      Safety

      • Keep antennas clear of power lines.
      • End-fed wire ends carry high RF voltage.
      • Observe RF exposure limits at high power.
      • All lengths, heights and impedances are rules of thumb. Measured values are more reliable.
